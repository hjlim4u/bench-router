# Gemini 4 Neon

**Gemini 4 Neon** (`gemini-4-neon`) is a frontier reasoning model released by Google DeepMind on **2026-11-18**. It sits between **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** and **Gemini 4 Carbon** for high-frequency complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows.

## Specifications & Pricing

- **Model ID**: `gemini-4-neon`
- **Context / Output**: 1,048,576-token unified input+output window; 65,536 default synchronous output, up to 1,048,576 with long decode.
- **Modalities**: Text, image, video, audio, and PDF inputs; text output.
- **Thinking levels**: `low`, `medium`, `high` (default), `xhigh`.
- **Pricing**: $3.00 / 1M input tokens ($0.15 cached, implicit only), $15.00 / 1M output tokens. Paid tier only.
- **Surfaces**: Gemini API (Google AI Studio), Gemini for business, Google Antigravity (standalone or Orchestrator planner + Hydrogen executor), Android Studio, Gemini app (Pro/Ultra), and Fairwind Program (Neon for Cyber).
