## I am once again asking

Prompt

Image 1 is the template. Replace the man in image 1 with the person in image 2. He must be clearly recognizable as the person in image 2, including face shape, skin tone, age, beard, glasses and hair. Keep everything else from image 1 exactly as is: expression, gaze, head pose, the brown winter jacket, lighting, the snowy street background, framing and the caption text "I am once again asking". Do not carry over the reference photo's expression, clothing or background.
