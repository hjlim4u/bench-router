Compare generation quality across media models

## Traffic light

Why we chose this prompt: Red, then green, then amber is not the real-world order. Models that lean on what traffic lights usually do will get it wrong, so this tests whether a video model follows a stated sequence.

Prompt

A fixed camera on a single traffic light against a plain grey sky. The light starts red, changes to green, and then changes to amber, in that exact order, each colour holding for a clearly visible moment. No other objects, no camera movement, no cuts.
