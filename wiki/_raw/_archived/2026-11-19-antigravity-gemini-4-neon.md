Gemini 4 Neon in Google Antigravity

The Antigravity Team·Nov 18, 2026·3 min read

Share
Copy linkShare on XShare on BlueskyShare on LinkedInShare

Today, we’re rolling out Gemini 4 Neon inside Google Antigravity. Bringing frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion, Neon hits the sweet spot between Gemini 4 Carbon for your deepest long-horizon projects and Gemini 4 Hydrogen for rapid everyday execution.
Built for demanding daily workflows
Most coding and knowledge work sits right in the middle: complex enough to need frontier-level reasoning, yet frequent enough to demand speed and efficiency on every turn. Gemini 4 Neon is engineered for real-world AI coding and agent harnesses, handling repo-level edits, debugging, refactoring, secure code generation, grounded enterprise analysis, and STEM reasoning with reliable multi-step instruction following and our lowest fabrication rate yet in internal Google Antigravity testing.
Frontier-level coding and agentic performance: Neon ranks #3 overall with a score of 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its Extra High thinking level and ranks #1 overall on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (58.6) and Zapier’s AutomationBench (56.3%).
Leaner tokens and four thinking levels (Low to Extra High): Alongside a high Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score, Neon at its default High thinking level shows a 32% lower cost per task than Carbon. Tune reasoning depth from quick interactive edits on Low to deep multi-step problem solving on Extra High, keeping agent loops responsive all day.

Using Gemini 4 Neon with /swarm, Antigravity scaffolds a multi-level 2-player game that runs in the built-in browser while the agent plays as the second player.
Pair Neon and Hydrogen with Orchestrator
Even with this model sweet spot, not every step in a multi-step task needs the same level of reasoning intelligence. Alongside Neon, Orchestrator in Google Antigravity now supports pairing Gemini 4 Neon as the planner with Gemini 4 Hydrogen as the executor in a single session.
Instead of manually switching models mid-task, Orchestrator uses Neon for upfront planning, architecture, and complex reasoning while handing off routine code edits, package updates, and test runs to Hydrogen. If Hydrogen hits friction, it hands back to Neon to inspect state, repair the plan, and resume execution. You get Neon’s judgment where it matters and Hydrogen’s speed and token efficiency everywhere else, stretching your quota further on every run.
Getting started
Gemini 4 Neon brings frontier-level intelligence and speed to demanding daily coding and agentic workflows. When you want Neon’s planning paired with Hydrogen’s speed without managing model switches, Orchestrator puts both models behind one prompt. Starting today, Gemini 4 Neon is rolling out across paid Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra subscribers in the Antigravity desktop app, Antigravity CLI, and Antigravity IDE extensions, alongside Orchestrator support pairing Neon and Hydrogen. Enterprises can access Gemini 4 Neon in Google Antigravity through Gemini for business.
