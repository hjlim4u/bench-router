Media benchmarks

Compare generation quality across media models

## Full wine glass

Why we chose this prompt: Almost every wine photo in training data shows a glass a third full, so models default to it. Filling the glass to the rim tests whether a model follows the prompt or the prior.

Prompt

A wine glass filled to the brim with white wine, the surface level with the rim of the glass, standing upright on a plain wooden table. Straight-on eye-level shot, glass upright and not tilted, no other objects in the frame.
